Situation:
I completed a LinkedIn Learning course titled “How to Make a Positive First Impression with a Hiring Manager” led by career coach Brittany Hayles. The course focused on practical methods to help candidates stand out in interviews through improved communication, preparation, and confidence.

Task:
My aim was to refine my interview skills—specifically to learn how to present myself effectively, tailor my answers to each role and company, and establish genuine connections with hiring managers. I also wanted to better understand how first impressions influence trust and rapport during interviews.

Action:
Throughout the course, I practiced creating a strong elevator pitch, researching potential employers, and customizing my responses to highlight the most relevant skills and experiences. I learned how professional body language, tone, and thoughtful follow-up communication contribute to a positive interview experience. Another focus area was learning how to ask meaningful questions to demonstrate genuine curiosity and alignment with the company’s goals.

Result:
After completing the course, I became noticeably more confident and articulate in interviews. I learned to structure my responses strategically, remain composed under pressure, and convey enthusiasm authentically. The training taught me that successful interviewing goes beyond memorized answers—it’s about preparation, self-awareness, and meaningful engagement with the interviewer.

Situation:

Task:
My goal was to demonstrate interview readiness by responding clearly and confidently using the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method while maintaining professionalism and self-awareness throughout.

Action:
Prior to the mock interview, I reviewed common interview questions and selected examples from my experiences that best demonstrated my abilities. I practiced structuring my responses for clarity and flow while staying focused on key achievements. During the session, I concentrated on my posture, tone, and pacing to convey confidence. I also aimed to communicate my understanding of workplace expectations and how I could contribute effectively in a professional environment.

Result:
The feedback was highly positive, highlighting my communication clarity, confidence, and professionalism. It also helped me identify areas for growth, such as summarizing my points more efficiently and managing response time. The exercise provided valuable insight into real interview dynamics and increased my preparedness for future opportunities.
